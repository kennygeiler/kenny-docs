Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

This policy explains how Kenneth Geiler (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the telephone number +1 (973) 847-2495 and the related call-screening service (the “Service”). It applies to voice calls and SMS/MMS messages sent to or from that number.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third party, except with subprocessors acting solely to deliver the messaging service on our behalf.

Information we collect

  • Call and message metadata: caller or sender phone number, carrier and line type, date and time, call duration, and delivery status.
  • Call content: audio recordings and machine-generated transcripts of calls placed to the Service, together with any business identifiers stated by the caller (company name, callback number, website, offer terms).
  • Message content: the text of SMS/MMS messages sent to the Service, and of any reply we send.
  • Contact list entries: names and phone numbers the account owner adds to an allowlist so that known contacts are routed normally rather than screened.

How numbers are collected and how consent is obtained

The Service is primarily inbound. We obtain a phone number when the holder of that number places a call or sends a message to +1 (973) 847-2495 of their own initiative. That call or message is the basis on which we may respond.

Allowlist entries are supplied directly by the account owner from their own contacts. Outbound messages, where sent, are transactional replies to a person who first contacted the Service, or messages to a person who gave express permission to be texted. We do not buy, rent, or import purchased marketing lists, and we do not send promotional or marketing messages.

How we use information

  • To answer, screen, and route incoming calls and messages.
  • To identify the origin of unsolicited and pre-recorded calls, and to compile records of those calls for the account owner’s own legal and regulatory purposes.
  • To respond to the sender of a message.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and debug the Service.

We do not use call or message content for advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

Call recording notice

Calls to the Service may be recorded and transcribed, and callers are notified at the start of the call. If you do not consent to being recorded, hang up and contact us by email instead.

Messaging terms

  • Message frequency varies and depends on your own interaction with the Service. We do not send recurring campaigns.
  • Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for messages.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages from us. You will receive a single confirmation, after which we send nothing further.
  • Help: reply HELP, or email us, for assistance.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Who we share information with

We share information only with vendors that operate the Service on our behalf, and only to the extent needed to do so: our telecommunications carrier (Twilio Inc.), our voice-agent and transcription providers, our hosting and database provider, and, where applicable, a speech-synthesis provider. Each acts under contract and may not use the data for its own purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when the account owner submits records as evidence in a legal proceeding, a regulatory complaint (for example to the FCC or FTC), or a demand letter arising from calls placed to the Service.

Retention

Call records, recordings, transcripts, and messages are retained for as long as they may be relevant to a claim or regulatory complaint, and in any event no longer than the applicable statute of limitations, after which they are deleted. Allowlist entries are retained until removed by the account owner.

Security

Data is stored in a managed, access-controlled database and transmitted over TLS. Administrative endpoints require a credential. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, and you may ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days. We may retain records that are the subject of, or reasonably anticipated to be relevant to, a legal claim.

Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
